About the Cosmic Mythology AR Explorer

Bridging ancient storytelling traditions and modern observational astrophysics across world skies.

1. Credits & Attribution

The Explorer synthesizes observational data and cultural humanities across multiple public institutions and scholarly foundations:

  • Astrometry & Stellar Data: Bright Star Catalogue (5th Revised Ed.), ESA Hipparcos Mission (1997), and the ESA Gaia DR3 catalogue (2022) for accurate equatorial coordinates (α, δ), proper motions, spectral types, and parallaxes.
  • Space Science & Observatories: NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, Space Telescope Science Institute (Hubble / JWST), European Southern Observatory (ESO VLT), European Space Agency (ESA Euclid), and ISRO (Aditya-L1).
  • Cultural Research & Collections: Sacred Texts Archive, Tufts University Perseus Digital Library, Chinese Text Project (ctext.org), National Museum of Australia (Songlines), and historical manuscripts from the Bodleian and Louvre archives.

2. Astronomical Methods & Coordinate Computing

The application runs zero server-side computation. All calculations run client-side in the user's browser:

  • Local Sidereal Time (LST): Derived from Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time (GMST) at Julian date $JD$, corrected by the user's local geographic longitude $\lambda$: LST = (GMST + λ) % 360°.
  • Hour Angle & Horizon Elevation: Computed using spherical trigonometry: sin(Alt) = sin(φ) · sin(δ) + cos(φ) · cos(δ) · cos(HA), determining whether an asterism is currently above the local horizon tonight.
  • Planisphere Projection: Stereographic polar projection mapping Right Ascension to radial polar angle and Declination to radial distance.
  • Keplerian Orbits: Solved in real time with circular approximations and eccentricity scaling for Mercury through Neptune.

3. Story Editorial Review & Cultural Heritage Policy

Our catalog contains 165 curated stories across 10 global traditions. Because sky lore encompasses sacred living oral traditions as well as ancient digitized literature, we apply strict editorial tiers:

  • Verified (Direct Text Evidence): The referenced digitized page or critical edition explicitly contains the narrative of the constellation or star.
  • Probable (Scholarly Context): The historical work, author, and chapter are confirmed in peer-reviewed catalogues, but the direct full-text reading requires archival access or print borrowing.
  • Pending Community Review Safeguard: Several Indigenous traditions (including Indigenous Australian, Polynesian wayfinding, and Indigenous Americas oral histories) embody living sacred lore. These are marked pending community review in our database and, per data/policy.json, are excluded by default in public builds until community representatives audit their presentation.

4. How to Report a Mistake or Cultural Correction

We welcome scholarly reviews, community elder feedback, and corrections regarding astrometry, translations, or cultural representations.

Reporting Guidelines:

  • For stellar coordinate or spectral bugs, cite the relevant catalogue identifier (HIP, HD, or Gaia DR3 source).
  • For cultural heritage adjustments, note the tradition key (e.g., ind, nak, poly) and provide preferred community wording or literature references.
  • Open an issue on the project repository or submit a pull request against data/myth.json or data/citation-audit.csv.
